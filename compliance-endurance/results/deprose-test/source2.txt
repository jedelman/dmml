The blacksmith of Ashgrove has a new problem this week: a merchant from
downriver offered to sell her ore at triple the going rate, since he knows
she has nowhere else to buy it now that the local quarry shut down. Her
father used to warn her about merchants like that. He was a smith himself,
back before his hands gave out, and he still remembers what it was like to
be squeezed by someone who controls the only supply of something you need.
