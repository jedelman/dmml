# Citation Verification Pass — Wittgenstein/Nietzsche, Symbolic Representation as Stratification (2026-08-25)

For a new bridging section connecting the two papers: the claim that DMML's
own discrete, symbolic representational commitment (the subject of the
world-model paper) is itself a stratum in the ontology paper's D&G sense.
Grades use the project's standard scale.

## 1. Wittgenstein, *Tractatus*, picture theory and 5.6 — ✅ Verified

5.6: "The limits of my language mean the limits of my world" (confirmed
against both Ogden and Pears/McGuinness, near-identical rendering; German
"Die Grenzen meiner Sprache bedeuten die Grenzen meiner Welt"). Picture
theory (2.1–2.225): "We make to ourselves pictures of facts" (2.1); "The
picture is a model of reality" (2.12); "What every picture... must have in
common with reality in order to be able to represent it at all... is the
logical form, that is, the form of reality" (2.18). Real, direct support for
"a symbolic system can only depict what shares its own articulated logical
form — form is the precondition of sense, not neutral transcription."
**Caveat**: the Tractatus itself does NOT frame this as falsification or
loss — that's a Nietzschean addition, not a Tractarian one. Use for the
coding-as-precondition half of the claim only.

## 2. Later Wittgenstein (*Philosophical Investigations*) — ⚠️ complicates a clean reading, state honestly

Explicitly repudiates the Tractatus's single-logical-form assumption in
favor of many local "language-games" embedded in "forms of life" — closer
to multiplicity/anti-arborescence than to a single stratifying operation.
**Real, sourced complication**: Deleuze's own recorded stance toward
Wittgenstein was hostile (Sayapin, "Philosophy after Wittgenstein: Deleuze's
Critical Project," *Philosophical Thought*) — framed as language-game
analysis "policing"/therapeutically containing thought against Deleuze's
own commitment to concept-creation and deterritorialization. Any
Wittgenstein/D&G connection is this paper's own synthesis, not one D&G's
reception of Wittgenstein licenses — state this plainly if he's invoked.

## 3. Nietzsche, "On Truth and Lies in a Nonmoral Sense" — ✅ Verified, strongest anchor

"What then is truth? A mobile army of metaphors, metonyms, and
anthropomorphisms... truths are illusions about which one has forgotten
that this is what they are; metaphors which are worn out..." (Kaufmann
translation, *The Portable Nietzsche*). Concept-formation: "Every concept
originates through our equating what is unequal" (Breazeale translation,
"Philosophy and Truth"). Two-stage argument: concept-formation discards
individual, non-repeating differences to manufacture a fixed general
category; the constructed/metaphorical origin is then forgotten, so the
concept is mistaken for neutral report rather than recognized as a coding
operation. Best textual anchor of the four checked — close to D&G's own
capture/coding vocabulary in almost the right register already.

## 4. Nietzsche→D&G strata lineage — 🚫 not supportable as scholarly lineage; real disconfirmation found

D&G's own cited source for double articulation/strata is explicitly **Louis
Hjelmslev** (structural linguistics — form/substance/content/expression,
first/second articulation), named directly in "Geology of Morals" ("the
Danish Spinozist geologist"). Nietzsche is not D&G's own cited source for
this machinery. Any Nietzsche/strata connection must be framed as this
paper's own thematic reading (a real, defensible resonance), never as
recovered scholarly consensus or something D&G's text itself traces.

## 5. Existing scholarship connecting Wittgenstein to D&G's strata/plane-of-consistency — 🚫 gap, not proof of absence

One adjacent source (Sayapin, above) discusses Wittgenstein vs. Deleuze
metaphilosophically and touches D&G's deterritorialization vocabulary, but
not confirmed (abstract-level access only) to engage strata/double-
articulation specifically. Open-web search only — a real gap to flag, not
to claim as settled absence.
