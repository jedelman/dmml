# Adversarial Review, Round Five — Plateau-by-Plateau (2026-08-25)

`stealth/ox-alpha`, fed the real page-cited primary-text findings gathered
across six plateaus (Rhizome, Geology of Morals, Smooth and Striated,
Apparatus of Capture, the BwO plateau, Of the Refrain) alongside the current
text of both papers, and asked to check the papers' use of each plateau
specifically against the verified quotes rather than re-litigate internal
logic (four prior rounds already did that). Findings and fixes:

1. **Rhizome — missing real material.** Asignifying rupture ("a rhizome may
   be broken, shattered at a given spot, but it will start up again on one
   of its old lines") is the named principle that already states the
   paper's own best argument (disputes persist rather than get converged
   away) — cited, but the paper had been making the argument in its own
   words without the primary-text principle that names it. Fixed: added as
   a fourth named principle, with "antigenealogy" also added, and an
   explicit tie-back where the paper makes that argument.

2. **Geology of Morals — factual misstatement, same error class as rounds
   one through four.** The paper called absolute deterritorialization "a
   rarer" case. The primary text says the opposite: "It is the strata that
   are always residue, not the opposite" (p. 56) — absolute deterritorial-
   ization is ontologically primary, not a scarce escape from a stratified
   default. Fixed: corrected, and the paper's own hedged candidate (the
   full commit log as plane-of-consistency-like) is now framed as more
   coherent under the corrected picture, not less.

3. **Geology of Morals — conceptual overclaim.** Four individual pantheon
   commits were called four full strata each serving as substratum for the
   next — inflating commit-scale units past what D&G's geological-scale
   strata are sized like. Fixed: rescaled using the primary text's own
   tool for sub-stratal units — epistrata/parastrata — with the world's
   whole fact-layer as one stratum and the individual commits as its
   epistrata, "strata in their own right" without needing to be full
   strata.

4. **Apparatus of Capture — correct to decline, but an unacknowledged
   exposure.** Both papers use "capture" pervasively (licensed by Geology
   of Morals' own stratic sense), but neither previously acknowledged that
   ATP has a separate, later plateau where "capture" means something
   unambiguously extractive (rent, profit, tax) that this paper does not
   import. Silence read as either not knowing about Plateau 7 or evading
   it. Fixed: one explicit disclosure sentence added, naming the other
   plateau and stating the borrowing is stratic-capture-only.

5. **Smooth and Striated — solid, one cheap addition.** "Never believe
   that a smooth space will suffice to save us" (p. 500) gives the paper's
   existing anti-triumphalism a second plateau's authority, preempting the
   "romanticizing non-convergence" objection. Added.

6. **BwO plateau — solid**, no fix required beyond an optional, cost-free
   addition ("not with a sledgehammer... a very fine file," p. 160) paired
   with the quote already used. Added.

7. **Of the Refrain — solid, one omission worth taking deliberately.** "A
   territory is always en route to an at least potential deterritorial-
   ization" (pp. 320–321) complicates the "stabilizes the boundary" framing
   but also fits DMML better than a closed-vocabulary reading would, since
   extensibility is a real, checked design property. Added, framed as a
   feature of the reading rather than a gap in it.

Overall verdict: strongest round yet — every fix from rounds one through
four held under this new, primary-text-driven check — but two real,
verified-primary-text-contradicting errors were still found (items 2 and
3), the same class of error that has been fatal in every prior round when
left unfixed. Both corrected in this pass.
