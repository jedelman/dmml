# Sections XIV-XV: two major threads fuse before the Epilogue (2026-08-25)

`dmml/examples/benjamin_section_xiv_xv.rs` is the biggest payoff file in
this series so far — six checks, three of them genuine cross-section
predictions, all confirmed.

**Dada is the essay's first deliberate case of aura-destruction.** Every
prior section (II, IV, VI) described aura's decline as something
happening TO art — a historical/technological process. Dada is shown
doing the same thing to itself, on purpose, as strategy ("they branded
[their works] as reproductions with the very means of production").
Modeled with an explicit `agency: "deliberate"` fact, checked to name
this distinction rather than let it blur into the essay's other
aura-decline material.

**Duhamel appears twice, in two different citation postures — a new
one, and a partial variant.** His structural observation ("my thoughts
have been replaced by moving images") is treated as accurate despite his
open hostility to film — content and evaluative stance pulled apart and
judged separately, a posture this series hasn't modeled before. His
second, more hostile quote ("a pastime for helots...") gets a related
but distinct treatment: cited, then explicitly dismissed as "the same
ancient lament... a commonplace" — correct as an instance of a
known complaint, therefore analytically useless. Checked in the produced
content (contains "commonplace"), a seventh posture in this project's
running tally.

**Two predictions confirmed under real checks, not just noted as
resemblances:** Section XV's "quantity has been transmuted into quality"
genuinely `consumes` Section V's `qualitativeShift` fact (Check 2) — the
verbatim echo is a real citation. And architecture's "prototype of a
work of art... consummated by a collectivity in a state of distraction"
genuinely consumes Section XII's `simultaneousCollectiveCapacity` fact
(Check 5) — Section XII's "architecture, epic, film, not painting" claim
and this section's architecture-as-paradigm claim are the same fact
appearing twice, not two independent observations about the same
building type.

**The real point of the file: the essay's two major threads fuse at its
own penultimate claim.** "The public is an examiner, but an absent-
minded one" is checked to genuinely consume BOTH Section VIII's
testing-posture fact AND this section's own distraction material
(Check 6) — not a fresh coinage arriving independently right before the
Epilogue, but the explicit synthesis of critic-without-personal-contact
(VIII) and distraction (XIV-XV) into one final formulation.

Also modeled, more simply: the concentration/distraction chiasmus as one
commit producing two facts with a real reversed direction (viewer enters
the work vs. the mass absorbing the work), and buildings' twofold
appropriation (touch/sight) flagged explicitly `symmetry: "asymmetric"`
— habit, the tactile side's own mode, governs the optical side too,
unlike the essay's earlier, more evenly balanced twofolds (Sections II,
III, VIII).

Only the Epilogue is left.
