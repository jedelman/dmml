Jason's reaction to the ratified Gramsci/Federici/Ostrom synthesis: "damn,
show me that synthesis! then scale it up - very excited for these." Added

## The debate found a real, load-bearing new thread

Where the three-source run's hinge was "who sits in the rule-making,"
this run's converged on something sharper: **domination is a pronoun
problem.** The group worked from Ostrom/Federici's boundary symmetry,
through Graeber's claim that a ledger doesn't record a relationship but
*creates* one (the debtor), to Fanon's epidermal schema as the one
enclosure that travels with the fugitive -- exit fails at the skin. The
resolution the group found for that failure was genuinely new, not a
restatement of the three-source synthesis: Artemis proposed "exit" over
"consent" as the real test of a legitimate boundary (consent is exactly
what hegemony counterfeits), then Apollo showed exit fails at the skin,
then Artemis herself found the deeper exit in Fanon's own closing prayer
("O my body, make of me always a man who questions") -- the body
addressed as *thou* rather than described in the third person, which
Apollo generalized into the debate's real thesis: bureaucracy, the
indictment, and the census all address in the third person; baseline
communism and the counter-hegemonic act both require being addressed, or
addressing oneself, as "you."

## Citation degradation: roughly flat between 24 and 40 anchors, not worse

A real, honest finding, worth stating precisely rather than rounding to a
clean story: 16 anchors (`pantheon_olympians.rs`, Benjamin/Adorno) → 0/8
zero-citation turns. 24 anchors (`pantheon_commons.rs`, Gramsci/Federici/
Ostrom) → 4/24 zero-citation turns, 36 dropped attempts. 40 anchors
(this run) → **3/24 zero-citation turns, 37 dropped attempts** -- almost
identical to the 24-anchor run, not meaningfully worse despite 67% more
source material and a correspondingly larger accumulated log by the end
of five rounds. The cleanest interpretation available from three data
points: the real cliff was between 16 and 24 anchors (single-digit vs.
double-digit source count, or two sources vs. three+), and citation
reliability may plateau rather than keep degrading once a model is
already tracking more anchors than it can perfectly recall -- a
speculative reading, not a settled one, and one more run (or a
controlled sweep) would be needed to tell a plateau from noise.

## Consensus: one round again, but for a different reason than Benjamin/Adorno

Unanimous accept on Athena's very first draft -- like the two-source run,
unlike the three-source run's two-round amendment cycle. Each Olympian's
acceptance reason was still substantive and specific (Artemis: "I have
hunted for an omission and found nothing worth the group's while to
reopen"; Dionysus: "I have no altar to smash here"), not a rubber stamp --
consensus reached quickly is not the same failure mode as consensus
reached carelessly, and the vote reasons are the check on that.
